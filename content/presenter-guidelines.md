*Revised: August 14, 2026*

The UMBC Linux Users Group (LUG) welcomes presenters of all skill levels! That being said, we want to help you deliver a clear, engaging, and accessible talk. 

If your goals or setup don’t fully align with these guidelines, don’t worry! Reach out to us at **lug@umbc.edu** or talk to an officer we are happy to make accommodations. We don't want any of these requirements to discourage you from presenting

## 1. Deadlines & Deliverables

Please email the following details to **lug@umbc.edu**:

### At *least* 3 days prior to the meeting:
* **Meeting Title:** For club announcements and schedule.
* **Brief Synopsis:** A 1–2 sentence summary of what your talk will cover for us to write better announcements.
* **Speaker Info (Optional):** How you’d like your name displayed and links you'd like us to include (GitHub, personal website, etc.).

\* We make meet announcements on the following platforms: [myUMBC](https://my3.my.umbc.edu/groups/lug), [Club Website](https://lug.umbc.edu/blog) and Discord.

### By 11AM day-of your presentation.
* **Slides (PDF format):** PDF guarantees cross-platform compatibility and distribution.
* **Publishing Permissions:** Clarify what content we have permission to publish on our website. (e.g., slides, code samples, ). See an [example meeting post](https://lug.umbc.edu/blog/05-6-2026-meeting/).

## 2. Presentation Guidelines

* **Slide Design:** Keep text minimal on each slide—use slides to support your speaking points, not as a script. Focus on high contrast and large, readable fonts.
* **Time Commitment:** Plan for roughly **30–45 minutes** of presentation, followed by **10–15 minutes** for Q&A (unless arranged otherwise).
* **Live Demos:** We strongly suggest that you test and rehearse any live demonstrations beforehand.
* **Terminal & Code Displays:** If displaying a terminal or code editor, use a large font size and a high-contrast color scheme suitable for a classroom environment.

## 3. Room & AV Setup

* **Connectors:** LUG meetings aren't always held in the same classroom so the Room/AV setup may vary. Typically we expect HDMI to be available. If you can't interface with this please reach out to an officer and we can provide an adapter. Alternatively, if you aren't doing any demonstrations you may request the slideshow be ran on one of our systems.
* **Screen Resolution:** Slides formatted in **16:9 widescreen** work best with the displays / projectors commonly found in classrooms.
